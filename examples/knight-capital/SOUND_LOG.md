# Sound log: Knight Capital example

This example uses only the shipped library: the ElevenLabs effects and bed in `template/sfx` and `template/music` (prompts in `template/sfx/PROMPTS.md`) and the synthesized utility sounds in `template/sfx/basic`. No sounds were generated for it in this repo. Every placement is in `sound.json`; the rendered placements are written to `out/sfx_placements.json` (79 placements in 39.5 s, 23 of them the speeding-up e-mail pings).

Difference from the original approved mix: the original used 12 utility sounds (whooshes, impact, typing, click, glitch, swipe, riser, thump, ticks, keyboard, pop) from a private library that can't be redistributed. Here they are synthesized stand-ins matched to the originals' length and loudness, so the balance is the same but they don't sound identical.
