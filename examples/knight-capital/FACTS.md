## Dramatised lines

- S1 "Ninety-seven emails?" repeats F10 as a reaction (D).
- S3 "Oh. So it's off, then." is a naive misreading by a commentator character, corrected at once by the narrator ("It wasn't." = F5). No claim about Knight staff.
- Power Peg waking on "Over" is the visual metaphor for the old code being used once trading began (D, next to F5/F6). Round 3 cut the spoken "and it woke up"; the picture carries it.

## Round 3 script changes (natural-speed re-voice: words cut, no fact changed)

101 → 82 words. Spoken date cut (F1 stays on the opening tag). "On that one, old code called Power Peg was still there" → "That one still had old code called Power Peg" (same F5). "At nine thirty, the market opened, and it woke up" cut from the voice (F6 on the clock and bell, the metaphor in the picture). The title question drops its number ("cost that much?"); the title card says "$460 MILLION" right after (F2/F9).

## People

- The technician is unnamed in the order: drawn only from behind, never named.
- Dot and Gus are this skill's own commentator characters, not Knight employees and not real people.
- "Power Peg" is drawn as a sleepy old computer: the code, not a person. The inbox sender is shown as a generic "SYSTEM" (the order's sender details aren't in the quote).
